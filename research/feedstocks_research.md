# Feedstocks Research

Hypothesis: stocks that rise rapidly share a pattern. A CANSLIM-style screen picks them
when quarterly NP is about ₹100 Cr, or when a single quarter's NP approaches the prior
annual NP. Positive news then tends to follow entry.

> Data status: findings below come from web-search summaries and are NOT yet verified
> against BSE/NSE filings or Screener. Entries marked (?) are unconfirmed.

## Common pattern
1. Policy- or capex-backed structural theme (semiconductors/electronics x3, AI power x1).
2. Earnings inflection visible in the latest quarter.
3. Base-and-breakout on the chart; pullbacks hold the rising 20-EMA.
4. Headlines arrive after price starts moving.

## Candidate screen (to backtest)
- Latest quarterly NP >= ~100 Cr OR >= ~30% of prior-year NP.
- YoY quarterly PAT growth > 50%.
- Stock belongs to a policy/capex theme with recent government or large-customer catalyst.
- Price above rising 20-EMA after a multi-month base; breakout on volume.
- Institutional holding rising (verify on Screener).
